The fallback ladder

Not every publisher sends a trailer. This is what each concept renders depending on what asset actually comes in — from full motion down to a placeholder with just a title.

1 · Trailer
Cyberpunk 2077. publisher sent: trailer mp4 → we render: autoplay muted loop (static frame if motion reduced)
2 · Screenshots
Stardew Valley. publisher sent: 3 store screenshots, no trailer → we render: slow crossfade loop
3 · Key art
Elden Ring. publisher sent: key art only → we render: Ken Burns pan/zoom, 18s alternate
4 · Portrait
Baldur's Gate 3. publisher sent: portrait cover only, wrong aspect → we render: cover centred over a blurred fill of itself
